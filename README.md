I am particularly focused on uncovering biological heterogeneity at the single-cell level and integrating multi-omics data to elucidate regulatory mechanisms in complex biological systems. In addition, I work extensively on the processing and quantitative analysis of high-throughput biological images, including 2D and 3D microscopy data. I am proficient in R and Python, with solid skills in data processing, statistical modeling, and visualization
